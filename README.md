> *"In a society that judges you by your cover, what happens when you get a second body?"*

**Lookism** is a South Korean animated series on Netflix adapted from the hit webtoon by **Park Tae-joon** and produced by *Studio Mir*. It follows **Park Hyung-suk**, an overweight and bullied high school student who mysteriously wakes up in a second, conventionally handsome and athletic body, forcing him to live a double life between day and night.

### Why It Stands Out
* **Dual-Identity Storytelling:** Explores the stark contrast in how society treats the exact same person based solely on physical appearance.
* **Fluid Action & Animation:** *Studio Mir* delivers crisp fight choreography and expressive character emotion.
* **Music & Culture:** Features standout vocal performances and original tracks, including *"Fly Up"* performed in the school festival arc.

### Main Characters
1. **Park Hyung-suk (Daniel Park)** – The protagonist navigating life with two bodies.
2. **Lee Jin-seong (Zack Lee)** – A hot-headed boxer learning humility and loyalty.
3. **Lee Eun-tae (Vasco)** – A formidable fighter with a strict moral code against bullies.
4. **Pyeon Deok-hwa (Duke)** – An aspiring rapper who refuses to give up despite constant discrimination.
